Better briefs

Include the context suppliers need.

Useful accessibility sourcing requests may include measurements, photographs, drawings, occupancy constraints, project location, expected timing and the relevant professional assessment. SiteSupply’s request form creates a starting point without replacing code review, design or professional advice.

Important boundary. SiteSupply is a sourcing and coordination platform. It does not certify compliance, provide architectural or engineering advice, or replace inspections and permits.
