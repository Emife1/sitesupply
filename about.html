Our purpose

A practical bridge between site demand and supplier capability.

SiteSupply is being built to make construction sourcing clearer, more structured and more accessible for Ontario projects and suppliers.

Origin

Built from overlapping real-world experience.

SiteSupply’s direction grew from experience across care environments, disability support, accessibility-related facility needs, marketing, design and local coordination. Those fields share a common challenge: outcomes depend on finding the right provider, communicating the requirement clearly and managing details that are easy to lose.

The platform applies that lesson to construction supply. Instead of beginning with scattered calls, incomplete messages and opaque comparisons, SiteSupply begins with a structured project request.

The goal is not to replace supplier expertise. It is to make qualified demand easier to understand, route and respond to.

What SiteSupply is becoming

  • A trusted intake layer for construction materials and site services.
  • A verified supplier discovery network focused first on Ontario.
  • A project workspace for requests, responses and sourcing context.
  • A stronger digital path for accessibility-focused construction needs.

What SiteSupply will not do

SiteSupply will not publish fabricated ratings, disguise planning estimates as supplier quotes or sell rankings without disclosure. Supplier availability, pricing and contract terms remain the responsibility of the relevant supplier and customer.
