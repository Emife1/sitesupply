Common questions

Clear boundaries from the beginning.

Does SiteSupply provide the final price?

No. Final pricing and commercial terms are supplied by participating suppliers. SiteSupply structures and routes requests.

Is the service limited to Ontario?

The first network focus is Ontario, with support for relevant Canada–US transport corridors.

Can suppliers join now?

Yes. Supplier applications are reviewed before public profiles are approved.

Is the workspace live?

The public request, supplier application and contact workflows are production-connected. Workspace planning values remain clearly labelled demonstrations until suppliers respond.
